About me

Where hardware meets intelligence

I'm a fourth-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the Faculty of Engineering, Benha University, specializing in Systems and AI Automation. I love the moment a model stops being a notebook and starts driving something real — a robot, a camera, a production line.

My recent work includes a YOLOv8 tracking system with persistent re-identification, a multithreaded industrial vision suite, a PID-controlled line-follower robot and a gesture-controlled robot car. Before that, I spent 1.5+ years as an AI Trainer at Outlier AI, helping improve large language models across 60+ projects.

Beyond engineering, I led finances as Treasurer of the IEEE Benha University Student Branch and represent the branch in regional and global IEEE programs.
